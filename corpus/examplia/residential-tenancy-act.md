## Section 5 — Deposits
A landlord may not take a deposit of more than two months' rent. The deposit
must be placed in an approved deposit scheme within 30 days of receipt.

## Section 6 — Return of deposit
At the end of a tenancy the landlord must return the deposit within 21 days,
less any deductions for unpaid rent or damage beyond fair wear and tear. Each
deduction must be itemised in writing.

## Section 14 — Notice to end a periodic tenancy
A landlord must give at least two months' written notice to end a periodic
tenancy. A tenant must give at least one month's written notice.

## Section 15 — Repairs
The landlord is responsible for repairs to the structure, exterior, heating,
hot water and sanitation. The tenant must report defects promptly.
