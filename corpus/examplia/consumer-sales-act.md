jurisdiction: EX
instrument: Examplia Consumer Sales Act 2019

## Section 3 — Goods to be of satisfactory quality
Goods sold by a trader to a consumer must be of satisfactory quality, fit for
any purpose the consumer made known before purchase, and as described.

## Section 7 — Short-term right to reject
A consumer who receives goods that do not meet section 3 may reject them and
receive a full refund if they notify the trader within 30 days of delivery.

## Section 8 — Repair or replacement
After the period in section 7, the consumer may require the trader to repair
or replace the goods once. If the repair or replacement fails, the consumer
may reject the goods and receive a refund, reduced to reflect use if the
goods were delivered more than six months earlier.

## Section 12 — Online purchases: cancellation
A consumer who buys goods online may cancel the contract for any reason
within 14 days of delivery. The trader must refund within 14 days of
receiving the goods back. Personalised goods and sealed hygiene goods that
have been unsealed are excluded.
